Everything from now to the first Thursday, one week at a time, plus how to hire, how registration works, and what happens every Thursday.

Hiring teachers and subs

  1. Share the link: firstalma-kdo.vercel.app/apply.html. Applications land on the Leaders tab of the sheet as Applied.
  2. Call within 2 or 3 days. Then call the reference.
  3. Short in-person interview, ideally with Josh there.
  4. Background check through the church before anyone is approved.
  5. Set their status to Approved or Sub. Every 2 approved teachers opens a room.
  6. Paperwork goes through the church bookkeeper: tax forms, the work eligibility form, and payroll.
  7. Training before January: Melissa’s training, CPR and first aid, Arkansas’s free online mandated reporter training, and safe sleep for anyone in the baby room.
  8. Keep 1 or 2 subs for every open room, not just enough teachers.

Good interview questions:

  • Tell me about a time you cared for young children.
  • A toddler bites another child. What do you do?
  • Someone you don’t recognize comes to pick up a child. What do you do?
  • A child cries for mom all morning. How do you help?
  • Why do you want to serve at KDO?

Registration and payments

  • Families register at firstalma-kdo.vercel.app starting December 1. Each child shows up on the Kids tab in the order they signed up.
  • A child with a spot shows Spot held. Text or email the family to welcome them, then set them to Confirmed once their first payment is in. Set Withdrawn if they drop out, and the next family on the waitlist moves up.
  • Families pay by the 7th of each month, January through May. One toddler or preschooler is $93 a month, one baby is $113. May is smaller since it has only 2 Thursdays.
  • 10% off for siblings. 10% off for paying the semester in full by January 7.
  • If a payment is more than 2 weeks late, the spot can go to the next family on the waitlist.
  • Decide with the bookkeeper how payments come in and where they’re tracked. The sheet doesn’t track payments.

Every Thursday

  • 8:30: staff arrive, set up, and pray together.
  • 9:00: doors open and families check in.
  • 2:00: pickup, tagged pickup only.
  • 2:30: rooms clean and back the way we found them.
  • Two adults in every room, all day. No adult is ever alone with a child.
  • Kids under 3 and kids 3 and up stay in separate rooms.
  • Send families a quick update in Homeroom. Only share photos of kids whose families said yes.

Every month

  • One fire drill and one tornado drill, written in the drill log every time.
  • Check who has paid by the 7th.
  • Look at the Leaders tab for new applicants and keep the sub list full.
