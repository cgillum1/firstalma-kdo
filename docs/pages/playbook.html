Hiring teachers and subs

Hire as many good people as you can. Tell each one up front: how many paid teachers we need depends on how many kids sign up, and everyone else will be a sub. You make the final call on who teaches every week and who subs.

  1. Applications from firstalma-kdo.vercel.app/apply.html land on the Leaders tab.
  2. Call within 3 days, then call their reference.
  3. Short interview, ideally with Josh there.
  4. Church background check before anyone is approved.
  5. Mark them Approved or Sub. Every 2 approved teachers opens a room.
  6. Before January: Melissa’s training, CPR and first aid, Arkansas’s free online mandated reporter training, and safe sleep for the baby room.

Interview questions:

  • Tell me about a time you cared for young children.
  • A toddler bites another child. What do you do?
  • Someone you don’t know comes to pick up a child. What do you do?
  • Why do you want to serve at KDO?

Spreading the word

  • In church: ask for a Sunday to speak, with your teachers up there with you. Put the KDO slide on the screen so people can scan the QR code.
  • Posters: church halls, the nursery, the library, coffee shops, pediatricians’ offices.
  • Bulletin and Facebook: share firstalma-kdo.vercel.app.
  • QR codes: for now they go to the test version of the website. Christian will update them when the site moves to the church’s real website, so they never land on the test site. Reprint the posters after that.

Registration and payments

  • Registration opens Sunday, November 15. Each child shows up on the Kids tab in sign-up order.
  • Spot held: text the family. Confirmed: set it once their first payment is in. Withdrawn: they dropped out, and the next family moves up.
  • Payments are due by the 7th, January to May: $92 a month for a toddler or preschooler, $112 for a baby. May is smaller.
  • 10% off for siblings and for paying in full. More than 2 weeks late, the spot can go to the waitlist.

Every Thursday

  • 8:30 staff arrive and pray. 9:00 check-in. 2:00 tagged pickup. 2:30 rooms clean.
  • Two adults in every room. No adult is ever alone with a child.
  • Kids under 3 and 3 and up stay in separate rooms.
  • Once a month: a fire drill and a tornado drill, written in the log.
